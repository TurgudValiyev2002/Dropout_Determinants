# Risk Clustering Report: Practical k=5

This solution clusters students using the whole dataset for practical intervention profiling.

It contains 720 students, has observed dropout rate 81.5%, mean predicted dropout probability 81.4%, tuition-not-up-to-date rate 29.3%, debtor rate 17.8%, and mean second-semester approved units 0.03. Recommended action: Mandatory academic advising and course-completion intervention.
